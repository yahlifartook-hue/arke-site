The -day return policy

  • Return any item within days of delivery for a refund of the item price. No reason needed.
  • Items must be unused or lightly used, with all accessories (cable, manual, packaging) and in secure packaging.
  • For change-of-mind returns the customer pays return postage. We recommend a tracked service. The original shipping fee is not refundable.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after we receive and check the parcel.
  • Want a different battery size or colour? Return the original and place a new order, or ask us about an exchange.

Faulty or damaged on arrival

If your fan arrives damaged, doesn’t work or isn’t what you ordered, tell us within days of delivery with your order number and a photo or short video. We will send a free replacement or a full refund including shipping, and you won’t need to post the faulty item back unless we ask.

Your statutory rights as a consumer are never affected by this policy.
