What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (like your browser’s local storage) are small pieces of data a website saves on your device. Some are essential for the site to work. Others are optional and only used if you agree.

Our store is built to keep optional tracking to a minimum: no analytics or advertising scripts load unless you opt in and the site owner has configured one.

Categories

Essential — always on

Needed for the shop to function: your cart, remembering your cookie choice, completing checkout securely. These do not require consent.

Preferences — optional

Remembers settings such as the colour you picked.

Analytics — optional

Helps us understand how the site is used (pages, devices, performance) so we can improve it. Loaded only after you accept.

Marketing — optional

Used to measure and personalise advertising. Loaded only after you accept.
