Delivery times

Every order is prepared and dispatched from our partner warehouses. Please allow:

  • Processing: 1–3 business days.
  • Delivery: typically 7–20 business days from dispatch depending on destination. Remote areas can take longer.

These are estimates. We will always tell you if something is running late.

Tracking

You’ll receive a tracking number by email once your parcel ships. Tracking updates can lag by a few days after dispatch while the parcel is scanned in.

Customs, duties and taxes

Depending on your country, your parcel may be subject to import VAT, duties or handling fees collected by customs or the carrier on delivery. These are not included in our prices and are your responsibility.

Addresses

Please double-check your address and phone number at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us immediately at . We cannot redirect parcels once they have been dispatched. Parcels returned because of an incorrect address may be re-sent at the customer’s cost.

Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel hasn’t arrived within 25 business days of dispatch, or arrives damaged, contact us with your order number (and photos for damage). We’ll replace it or refund you. See returns.
